## What this means for your channel

1. **Your benchmark set is cleaner than the general niche.** None of these four
   use fake news chyrons, "EXCLUSIVE" tags or AI fakes, and they still get
   millions of views. That's the lane to aim for, and it lasts longer.
2. **Steal the Final Leg format for opinion videos.** Giant condensed top text +
   one great photo + a question hook. It's easy to template so every video looks
   like your channel.
3. **Use TRP's "Situation" formula for breaking news**, and post fast.
4. **Use Jumpman's "proof" thumbnails for analysis videos.** Real frame + drawn
   line/circle showing the gap, plus a stakes title ("...Exposed a Huge Problem").
5. **Put yourself in the frame like Trenton**, with real expressions instead of
   fake shock, when the video is your take.
