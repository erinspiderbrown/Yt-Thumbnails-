# Track & Field Packaging Playbook

Built from vidIQ outlier research (Nov 2026): ~90 track & field videos that beat
their channel's average views by 2x–1000x, across three lanes — **controversy**,
**documentary/story**, and **opinion/commentary**. Reference thumbnails live in
`references/thumbnails/`, full data in `references/outliers.md`.

## Key insight

Small channels win this niche. Many of the biggest outliers come from channels
with 1K–30K subscribers (e.g. a 10.6K channel hit **4.3M views**, a 1.2K channel
hit **369K**). The packaging is doing the work, not the subscriber count.

## Thumbnail patterns

### 1. The "two-panel proof" layout (top performer)
Left: race action (finish line, runners falling behind, the subject ahead) + a
**red/green arrow** pointing at the subject. Right: a tight close-up of the
athlete's face. Bottom strip: **3–4 word statement in huge black condensed caps
on white**, often with a small red "EXCLUSIVE" tag.
- `moQImpFBM3U` — "HE'S NOT A HUMAN!" — 4.3M views, 40x
- `GLmy5X9zCng` — "IS HE EVEN HUMAN?" — 408K views, 1088x
- `v4dX8nYn5XI` — "HE'S NOT HUMAN" — 369K views, 110x

### 2. The lineup / rivalry shot (race-reaction lane)
Four athletes walking toward camera in national kits (USA vs JAMAICA),
names on bibs, **no text at all**. The kits and faces tell the story.
- `Ktq_Poh4TPE` 919K · `NYaD2ughuDk` 723K (150x) · `HbIt0LWRJKk` 254K (289x)

### 3. Emotional face + 2–3 word verdict (controversy / opinion)
One athlete, strong emotion (tears, scream, stare), red arrow at the face,
**yellow/white italic bold text** with black outline: "THIS IS SERIOUS!",
"SHE IS BACK!", "FINALLY!", "Banned Her..?".
- `W-MlBt6nK6Y` 49K on a 1.2K channel (15x) · `Y4zGc2rUFkM` 173K (53x)

### 4. Commentator reaction inset (your format)
Big scene on the left, **creator's shocked face** bottom-right (hand on head,
mouth open, pointing), 2–3 word text: "THEY HAD ENOUGH!", "THIS IS BAD".
- `Yy4ghPTXF4U` 463K · `NQAOHsel7PU` 169K · `kCE-mjlgrZU` 248K

### 5. Documentary / legacy look
Single athlete or older-vs-younger contrast, one dramatic word:
"TERRIBLE!", "TRAGIC DOWNFALL...". Darker grade, yellow highlight word.
- `MyMLWi0w0IY` 67K · `etaFiymvJ_s` 24K on a 1.2K channel (28x)

### Rules that show up in nearly every winner
- **≤ 4 words** on the thumbnail. Text must NOT repeat the title — it adds the emotion.
- **One clear subject**, face visible, emotion readable at phone size.
- **Arrows** (red, green or yellow) pointing at the thing to look at.
- **Palette:** red track + white/black text + one yellow accent word.
- Questions ("IS HE EVEN HUMAN?") and verdicts ("HE'S NOT HUMAN") both work.

### Don't copy these (even though some performed)
- **Fake news-channel graphics** (Fox News / CNN chyrons, a real anchor saying
  "VERY SAD NEWS"). That impersonates a real outlet/person, risks strikes for
  misleading metadata, and burns trust.
- **AI-faked scenes of real people** (crying, hospital beds) presented as real.
- **Stating doping or crimes as fact.** Frame as a question or "allegations"
  and back it up in the video. Defamation is a real risk in this niche.

## Your benchmark creators: house styles to borrow
Full breakdown in `references/creators/CREATORS.md`.

| Video type | Borrow from | Thumbnail | Title formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opinion / take** | The Final Leg | Giant tall condensed white caps across the top, athlete's head overlapping the text, one pro photo. Hook: "IS SHE HEALTHY?", "THERE'S A LOOPHOLE" | "What Everyone Missed About ___", "Is ___ Right About ___?", "Why EVERYONE Should Be Worried About ___" |
| **Breaking news / drama** | Total Running Productionz | Action photo + 2–4 white words top-left ("THIS IS A PROBLEM") + one curved arrow | "[Name] Situation Is Crazy", "...Keeps Getting Crazier", "People Are Worried About [Name]" |
| **Analysis / "how fast is this"** | Jumpman (3.7M, 2.4M, 2.1M views) | Real broadcast frame, little or no text, yellow line/circle drawn on the track | "I Don't Think You Understand Just HOW ___", "...Exposed a Huge Problem", "___ Finally Has A Serious Rival" |
| **You in the video** | Trenton Sandler | Your real face (tired, skeptical, curious), lowercase one-word text ("ouch.") | First person: "I Tried ___", "Did ___ Make ___?" |

**Main lesson:** none of these four use fake news graphics or "EXCLUSIVE"
tags, and they still beat the clickbait channels. Pick ONE house thumbnail style
(The Final Leg's is the easiest to template) so viewers recognise your videos.

## Title formulas

| Formula | Example from outliers | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **[Name] + "Just" + shocking result** | "Sam Blaskowski Just Became the Fastest White Man Alive — And Nobody Saw It Coming" | news / hot takes |
| **"This [Kid/Girl] Is DESTROYING..." + authority reacts** | "This Kid Is DESTROYING World Records – Even Bolt Is STUNNED!" | breakout athletes |
| **Bold prediction** | "Jakob Ingebrigtsen Will Never Win Another Title" | opinion |
| **"The Truth / What REALLY Happened"** | "The Tragic Truth About Flo-Jo's Legacy!", "What REALLY Happened to Allan Wells" | documentary |
| **Late confession** | "At 63, Ben Johnson Finally REVEALS What We All Suspected About Seoul 1988" | history / doping eras |
| **Expectation flip** | "The World Expected Gout Gout...Instead They Got Tate Taylor" | rivalries |
| **Country vs country** | "JAMAICA vs USA — The 200m Showdown That Shocked Everyone!" | race reaction |
| **"Is Back / Comeback"** | "TeeTee Terry's Comeback Could SHOCK The 100m World!" | returns |
| **Past catches up** | "Track Runner's Past Has Caught Up to Him Yet Again" | controversy |

Title rules:
- Put the **athlete's name first** when they're trending. Names are search terms.
- **1–2 CAPS words max** for emphasis (DESTROYING, REALLY, SHOCK). Not all caps.
- Open a loop ("...And Nobody Saw It Coming", "...Instead They Got").
- Keep it **under ~70 characters** so it isn't cut off on mobile.
