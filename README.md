# YT Thumbnails: Track & Field

Packaging lab for a track & field channel (documentaries, takes, controversy).

- `STYLE.md`: your house thumbnail style ("Photo Finish") and how to use it
- `tools/make_thumbnail.py`: thumbnail maker. Crops your real photo and adds the house style. Never edits faces
- `PLAYBOOK.md`: thumbnail and title patterns pulled from the niche's top outliers
- `references/`: outlier data and reference thumbnails (study only)
- `videos/NNN-slug/`: one folder per video with title options, thumbnails, and notes
